Our story

Building Trust, One Home at a Time

Bharadwaj Building Construction started in Jaggaiahpet, Andhra Pradesh, with a simple idea: build every home the way we would build our own — honest estimates, branded materials and work we are proud to sign our name to.

Today, with 15+ completed projects across Jaggaiahpet — from independent duplexes in Dollars Colony to gated-community homes at Bhuvanagiri Township — our reputation travels by word of mouth. Most of our new projects come from families who saw our work and asked who built it.

We take on a limited number of projects at a time, so every site gets personal attention from first visit to final handover.

  • Written agreement with stage-wise payments on every project
  • Engineer-supervised sites — no concreting without sign-off
  • Branded materials only — UltraTech cement, Vizag steel, Asian Paints
  • 1-year post-handover maintenance support, in writing

How it works

From First Visit to Final Handover

A clear, written process — so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Free Site Visit

    We visit your plot, understand your requirements, and study the site — soil, surroundings, access — before we quote anything.

  2. 2

    Plan & Transparent Estimate

    Floor plan, 3D elevation and an itemised per-sq-ft estimate. We revise the plan with you until you are fully satisfied.

  3. 3

    Agreement & Construction

    A written agreement with a stage-wise payment schedule. Branded materials, engineer supervision and weekly photo updates throughout.

  4. 4

    Handover & Support

    A snag-list walkthrough before the final payment — then 1 year of free maintenance support after handover.
