Our Services

Independent House Construction

Your own home, built from foundation to finishing by one accountable team. Single-floor houses, duplexes and villas — designed around your family's needs and your plot's Vastu.

We handle everything: soil testing, structural design, municipal approvals, construction and handover-ready finishing.

What's included

  • Turnkey construction from foundation to handover
  • Vastu-compliant floor plans on request
  • Structural design vetted by qualified engineers
  • Branded cement, steel and fittings throughout
  • Stage-wise payments linked to completed work
🏢

Commercial Buildings

Shops, offices and multi-storey commercial spaces engineered for strength and planned for rental value. We build to sanctioned drawings so your investment is approval-safe.

What's included

  • Shops, offices and G+2 / G+3 commercial blocks
  • Engineered structural drawings and soil study
  • Fire-safety and municipal approval guidance
  • Phased construction to suit your budget
  • Weekly progress reports with photographs
🔨

Renovations & Extensions

Give your existing building a new life — an extra room, an additional floor, a modern kitchen, or a full-home makeover. We assess the existing structure first, so extensions are safe.

What's included

  • Room additions and extra-floor construction
  • Structural safety assessment before extension
  • Kitchen and bathroom upgrades
  • Full-home renovation and repainting
  • Work planned to minimise disturbance at home

Planning & Design
